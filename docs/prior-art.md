# Prior art and novelty controls

**Audit date:** 2026-11-01. Primary articles and abstracts were checked for the central precedents below; this is **not** an exhaustive systematic review. An SSRN preprint is not peer-reviewed solely by being posted.

| Prior work | Relevant prior result | CDKA novelty constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Fox & Tishby (2016), [Minimum-Information LQG I](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01946), [II](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01947) | Sequential rate-distortion under control performance constraints; with/without memory | `min I(H;M) subject to decision performance` is not independently novel |
| Kolchinsky & Wolpert (2018), [Semantic information, autonomous agency and non-equilibrium statistical physics](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsfs.2018.0041) | Causal necessity of correlations for maintaining viability | Do not claim first causal/viability-based semantics |
| Murray (written 2026-09-07, posted 2026-09-14), [From Predictive State to Viable Action](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7427100) | Exact action-sufficiency via common safe actions, set cover and hypergraph coloring; pairwise conflicts insufficient | Our static B1 and higher-order compatibility are directly anticipated |
| Doyen & De Lara (2010), [Stochastic viability and dynamic programming](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sysconle.2010.07.008) | Chance-constrained viable feedback using dynamic programming | Recursive viability with exogenous capacities is not novel |
| Barandiaran, Di Paolo & Rohde (2009), [Defining Agency](https://doi.org/10.1177/1059712309343819) | Individuation, interactional asymmetry and normativity | Agency criteria must engage these antecedents |
| Klyubin, Polani & Nehaniv (2008), [Keep Your Options Open](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0004018) | Empowerment and information-theoretic potential influence | Distinguish potential influence from informed, sustainable control |
| Wissner-Gross & Freer (2013), [Causal Entropic Forces](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.110.168702) | Causal path-entropy objective yielding apparently intelligent effects | Do not mistake future path entropy for operational autonomy |
| Barnett & Crutchfield (2016), [Computational Mechanics of Input-Output Processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.2690) | Minimal predictive representations of input-output futures | Predictive sufficiency quotients have antecedents |
| Freidlin–Wentzell theory and Girsanov | Small-noise trajectory action and path-space change-of-measure identities | The basic barrier-to-bits link is an instrumental corollary, not a proven new theory |

## Required novelty audit for each claim

Record: exact theorem statement; assumptions and quantifiers; closest known equivalent; what the new hypothesis excludes or permits; proof or counterexample; implementation tests; confidence; and residual uncertainty. Avoid language such as "first", "novel theorem" or "universal agency" before an exhaustive check.

## Open prior-art targets

- Information-limited / rate-distortion viability and partially observed safety games.
- Observational equivalence versus intervention-based identification of autonomy.
- Self-maintaining controlled Markov processes and sensing/actuation degradation.
- Feedback coevolution, eco-evolutionary replicator systems, viable resource-constrained POMDPs.
- Coarse graining and compositional control over dynamically changing actuators.
