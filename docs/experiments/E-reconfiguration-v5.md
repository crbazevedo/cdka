## Scientific scope

Passing tests does not establish novelty. The safe-upgrade limit assumes a declared bounded fault support and a correct correction mechanism. Unbounded noise, noisy correctors, resource signaling of W, private persistent seeds and additional sensing are different model classes. No automatic conversion from normalized effort tokens to joules is made.
