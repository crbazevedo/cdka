# Independent review: certified program-family pruning

**Scope:** the inductive cover, maintenance boundary, compact decision
certificate and matched-output benchmark protocol. Reviewed sources:
[`program_families.py`](../../cdka/program_families.py),
[`program_family_sweep.py`](../../experiments/program_family_sweep.py),
[proof](program-family-proof.md) and
[proof-boundary tests](../../tests/test_program_families.py).

## Assessment

No scientific blocker was identified within the stated contract. The cover
preserves the informed per-preparation optimum, the optimal common minimax
regret, the optimal common maximin fidelity, and existence of a program meeting
both a regret limit and a fidelity floor. The result applies to preparations in
the certified sign cone and to the same prepaid common resource box.

The contribution is an executable inductive certificate that avoids generating
some complete programs. It does not establish a polynomial-time algorithm,
remove preparation enumeration, or preserve the full prediction interface.
Runtime acceptance depends on the separately recorded benchmark; no timing
numbers were independently reproduced in this review.

## 1. Coverage and the maintenance boundary

The implementation stores `q(w)=E K_w g`. Since the preparation contrast has
nonpositive coordinates, `q(f)<=q(p)` componentwise means that f is at least as
good as p. This is the sign-reversed version of the proof's `b=-q` convention.

The checker verifies `E K_a=A_a E` and nonnegativity of A for D/R. Thus an
existing dominance relation remains true under every D/R prefix. The grammar
induction is complete: a legal word either starts with D/R and has a suffix in
the preceding level, or starts with M and has an entirely ordinary suffix.
Both I and M are included at level zero.

Every no-M suffix is retained in sixteen physical coordinates. The optimized
maintenance map is the checked **four-by-sixteen** matrix `B=E K_M`; applying
it to that full suffix is exactly the original physical contraction. It does
not assert that M preserves the four-coordinate input domain. Pruning the
suffix library using its four-coordinate terminal response before applying M
would require a different proof and is not done.

The supplied cover assigns every generated candidate to a retained dominator.
Transitivity and the prefix identity cover the words omitted at earlier levels.
The verifier need not find a Pareto frontier, and a nonminimal valid cover is
acceptable. Physical correctness of the supplied kernels remains an inherited
obligation: the family hash binds their identity rather than deriving them from
physical events. `evaluate_frontier` separately rejects signatures outside the
nonpositive contrast cone.

## 2. Compact decision certificate

The compact certificate proves exactly its advertised decision outputs. Each
informed value B_h has an attaining plan and bounds every entry of its row.
For the claimed minimax value r, the selected program has regret at most r at
every preparation. For every candidate j, a supplied history i_j satisfies
`B[i_j]-V[i_j,j]>=r`. Therefore every candidate's worst regret is at least r,
while the selected candidate attains that lower bound.

The maximin argument is dual: the selected program stays above the claimed
value v on every row, and each alternative has a supplied row with value at
most v. These checks prove the two optimal values without searching again for
the optimizing programs. Ordered identities, exact values and scope are bound
by the digest; the inequalities and attaining witnesses establish the numerical
claims. Floats and booleans are rejected as exact rational certificate data.

This compact certificate does not publish each program's exact regret or all
admissible programs. Joint regret/fidelity feasibility is checked by a separate
scan over the retained response matrix. The experiment performs that scan;
feasibility cannot generally be inferred from the minimax and maximin programs
alone. The cover theorem makes the retained-matrix feasibility answer valid for
the full original library.

## 3. Independent exact checks

An independent check of the cover recurrence compared all retained responses
with full physical `word_value` evaluation and checked componentwise coverage
of every legal word at K=0,1,2,4. Informed values, minimax regret and maximin
fidelity also agreed with full enumeration for the four-preparation cohort.
The family checker passed while Pareto discovery was disabled.

| Ordinary updates K | Legal programs | Retained programs | Complete programs never generated by the pruned method |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 2 | 2 | 0 |
| 1 | 6 | 5 | 0 |
| 2 | 16 | 8 | 2 |
| 4 | 96 | 10 | 52 |

At K=4, 34 final candidates are distinct from the 44 complete programs actually
generated when the full no-M suffix library is counted. Subtracting 34 from 96
would overstate the avoidance. The finalized four-by-sixteen maintenance
contraction implements the same exact responses; its checked identity was also
audited directly in the code.

The finalized compact checker was independently exercised on a rational table
whose minimax and maximin programs differ. It accepted the expected certificate
with its compiler disabled, rejected six altered optimum claims, and the
evaluator rejected a preparation outside the sign cone. These targeted checks
are separate from the repository's larger regression sweep and CI results.

## 4. Cost-comparison audit

Both benchmark methods return the same outputs: informed values, one minimax
program and one maximin program. The unpruned baseline receives the same full
suffix sharing, four-coordinate D/R propagation and four-by-sixteen maintenance
contraction. It is not required to generate prediction envelopes or pay for a
family certificate that it does not need.

The ledger includes physical-kernel construction, preparation generation,
signature encoding, compilation, queries, decision-certificate construction,
serialization and checking. The pruned method additionally pays for its family
certificate and independent checker. Certificate parsing is inside the checking
measurements. Optional query-cache export is measured separately and excluded
from both decision-only totals.

The final setup protocol explicitly clears the raw-update, maintenance-row and
voter-profile caches before each physical-kernel sample. This avoids presenting
a warmed median as cold setup, including when the benchmark follows an exact
regression run in the same process. Historical timings using a different setup
protocol require alignment before direct comparison. The reported total is a
sum of component medians, not a measured median of complete process runs.

Serialized response/cache sizes do not measure peak resident memory. In
particular, the algorithm still constructs the full no-M suffix library.
Reduced leaf counts and final frontier sizes alone cannot establish a runtime
or memory advantage.

## 5. Limits that must remain attached to the result

- The decision guarantees are conditional on the declared reachable sign cone,
  legal grammar and common prepaid resources. Actual per-program execution cost
  can change under replacement; adding it as a constraint or objective requires
  a stronger dominance relation.
- The reduced library preserves an optimum, not every tied identity or
  uniqueness. The uninformative-sensor boundary makes all original programs tie.
- Removing dominated responses can change lower prediction envelopes. A
  prediction certificate on the representatives is not the earlier full-library
  prediction certificate.
- All `2^K` no-M suffixes and all explicitly generated `2^h` preparation histories
  remain. No polynomial bound on frontier size is established.
- Maintenance cannot generally be moved to the final position. The retained
  early-maintenance counterexample prevents interpreting one observed optimum
  as a universal scheduling rewrite.
