# Mathematical antecedents of the finite CDKA core

**Audit date: 2026-11-05.** Scope: the [integrated finite core](../strategy/realizable-renewable-core.md), its continuation-response construction, preparation/program covers, and the stochastic-realization boundary. Five primary works were inspected at the relevant full-text passages; a sixth historical source is explicitly abstract-only. This is a claim audit, not an exhaustive priority search. Daniel John Murray and semantic-information literature are outside this audit.

## 1. Assessment

The central mathematical instruments have close, explicit antecedents. Continuation-response spans, preservation relative to a chosen decision criterion, bisimulation, stochastic intertwining, and affine worst-case regret comparisons should be attributed as established tools. The inspected sources do not establish priority for the complete CDKA construction, and their contracts are not interchangeable with it.

The defensible CDKA contribution to assess is its **concrete synthesis and certification under one declared physical contract**: legal paid continuations; a controller with specified actual observations; preparation witnesses generated by actual histories; dominating representatives that are original executable programs; exact local certificates that compose across both families; and counterexamples identifying where a previously valid representation ceases to apply. These can be useful research contributions without a claim to a new general principle of sufficiency or minimax optimization. Historical priority for that precise combination remains unresolved.

|---|---|---|
| A finite span of continuation responses supports exact recursive prediction | S1, Theorem 1 and Lemma 1 | Same linear instrument; CDKA restricts probes and continuation grammar |

## 2. Primary-source evidence

### S1. Predictive Representations of State

**Michael L. Littman, Richard S. Sutton, Satinder Singh; NIPS 2001, Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 14.** [Official proceedings PDF](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/file/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Paper.pdf).

**Access and exact evidence.** Relevant full text inspected. §1, equations (1)–(3), represents predictions of action-observation tests through core predictions and a recursive normalized update. Theorem 1 bounds the number of core tests by the number of states in a minimal finite POMDP. §2 constructs independent outcome vectors by extending tests; Lemma 1 proves that their span contains every test outcome. The subsequent example explicitly permits negative linear coefficients. Section and theorem locators are preferable because this PDF contains intervening blank pages.

**Scope and CDKA comparison.** This is a direct antecedent for finite linear continuation closure, including the separation between linear coordinates and probability mixtures. Its prediction contract covers action-observation tests; §1 explicitly sets control and reward aside. CDKA's [maintenance proof](maintenance-enabled-signatures-proof.md), §§3–5, adapts the instrument to terminal task probes, a grammar permitting at most one paid maintenance call, and exact rank/word certificates. Neither linear closure itself nor signed coefficients support a new-general-representation claim.

### S2. Towards a Unified Theory of State Abstraction for MDPs

**Lihong Li, Thomas J. Walsh, Michael L. Littman; 2006, Ninth International Symposium on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics.** [Author-hosted paper](https://thomasjwalsh.net/pub/aima06Towards.pdf).

**Access and exact evidence.** Relevant full text inspected. §3.3, Definition 3, manuscript pp. 4–5, distinguishes preservation of the one-step model, all-policy Q-values, optimal Q-values, optimal actions with their values, and optimal actions alone. §3.4, Theorem 2, orders these abstractions. Theorem 3 states policy-lifting guarantees for the first four types and a failure possibility for the last.

**Scope and CDKA comparison.** Task- or decision-relative sufficiency is established territory. These are partitions of finite discounted MDP states, with an induced abstract model and lifted policies. CDKA instead evaluates one common complete program over admissible preparations, with observations and resource feasibility fixed separately. That distinction requires an explicit mapping; a guarantee for fully observed MDP policies cannot simply be transferred to the physical controller. This source also blocks conflating preservation of optimal actions with preservation of all continuation values.

### S3. Equivalence Notions and Model Minimization in Markov Decision Processes

**Robert Givan, Thomas Dean, Matthew Greig; 2003, Artificial Intelligence 147(1–2), 163–223.** [DOI: 10.1016/S0004-3702(02)00376-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(02)00376-4). [Author-hosted manuscript](https://cs.brown.edu/people/tdean/publications/archive/GivanetalAIJ-03.pdf).

**Access and exact evidence.** Relevant full text inspected in the 62-page manuscript. §3.3, manuscript pp. 12–14, defines stochastic bisimulation through rewards and block transition probabilities. Theorem 7, p. 14, states quotient equivalence and lifting of optimal policies; its proof is on p. 46. §4 and Theorem 8, proved on p. 47, distinguish a valid bisimulation from computation of the coarsest one.

**Scope and CDKA comparison.** The [v17 symmetry reduction](T4R-renewal-and-selected-memory.md), §5, uses the established compatibility idea. Its 48-state construction is a sufficient quotient for the declared kernels. To claim minimality would require a lower bound or a coarsest-equivalence argument with the same target properties. Moreover, the smallest bisimulation quotient and the smallest linear task-value representation solve different problems. The source supports these distinctions, not an identification of their dimensions.

### S4. Hitting Times and Interlacing Eigenvalues: A Stochastic Approach Using Intertwinings

**James Allen Fill, Vince Lyzinski; online 2012, Journal of Theoretical Probability 27, 954–981 (2014).** [DOI: 10.1007/s10959-012-0457-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10959-012-0457-9). [Author-hosted 2012 manuscript](https://www.ams.jhu.edu/~fill/papers/intertwinings.pdf).

**Access and exact evidence.** Relevant full text inspected. §1.1, Definition 1.1, manuscript p. 2, gives a stochastic link `Lambda P = P_hat Lambda` and compatible initial laws. The following consequences give all-time intertwining and law reconstruction. Equation (1.1) and p. 3 describe sample-path linking. §1.3, p. 4, treats signed quasi-links: algebraic identities remain, while the same stochastic linking construction is unavailable. §2.1, Proposition 2.1, pp. 4–5, constructs an invariant mixture family from block laws. The paper explicitly attributes earlier linking constructions to Diaconis and Fill (1990), §2.4.

**Scope and CDKA comparison.** `E K_a = M_a E` is this intertwining instrument. CDKA's `K_a = A_a E`, `M_a = E A_a` is a concrete sufficient factorization. A common `E` for all admitted actions, initial-law membership, and failure mass must still be checked. Controller observability does not follow from evaluator law reconstruction. This source is an accessible primary application and exposition, not the origin of intertwining.

### S5. Minmax Regret Combinatorial Optimization Problems with Ellipsoidal Uncertainty Sets

**André Chassein, Marc Goerigk; 2017, European Journal of Operational Research 258(1), 58–69.** [DOI: 10.1016/j.ejor.2016.10.055](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2016.10.055). [Institutional accepted manuscript](https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/id/eprint/82586/1/source.pdf).

**Access and exact evidence.** Relevant full text inspected. §2.1, manuscript p. 3, defines optimization and evaluation of worst-case regret. §3.1, p. 15, rewrites the epigraph using `z >= c^T x - c^T y` for every scenario and comparator, and explicitly discusses restriction to interval-set extreme points. Equation (4) recovers the scenario-specific optimum benchmark.

**Scope and CDKA comparison.** This is the same pairwise-affine instrument used by the [preparation proof](preparation-family-proof.md), §3. After reversing cost to value, each comparator difference is affine in the preparation signature. The source's main new results concern ellipsoidal uncertainty and combinatorial optimization; it is not cited as the origin of regret or convexity. CDKA's two-sided cover result is a composition with pointwise program domination and common physical feasibility. It should not be presented as a new general extreme-point theorem.

### S6. Markov Functions — historical source with limited access

**L. C. G. Rogers, J. W. Pitman; 1981, Annals of Probability 9(4), 573–582.** [DOI: 10.1214/aop/1176994363](https://doi.org/10.1214/aop/1176994363). [Author's publication list](https://www.skokholm.co.uk/lcgr/downloadable-papers/). [Author-uploaded record and abstract](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/38361843_Markov_Functions).

**Access and evidence limit.** Bibliographic entry and abstract inspected; original full text was not retrieved. The abstract states a sufficient condition for a function of a time-homogeneous Markov process to remain time-homogeneous Markov, with diffusion applications. No exact theorem number, hypothesis list or equivalence with the CDKA construction is claimed. S4 supplies the directly inspected finite intertwining identities. A theorem-level comparison with S6 remains open.

## 3. What follows mathematically, independently of priority

For `V_x(p) = c + x r_p`, fixed programs `p,q` give an affine difference. Thus, if `Q` consists of actual preparations and `conv(Q) = conv(X)`,

```text
sup_(x in X) max_(q in L) [V_x(q) - V_x(p)]
  = max_(q in L) max_(x in Q) [V_x(q) - V_x(p)].
```

The maximum over the finite comparator library commutes with the scenario supremum. This uses affine pairwise differences, not an affine informed maximum. Fixed-program minimum fidelity is preserved for the same reason. No minimax interchange of the controller and uncertainty player is involved.

If each original program `p` has one retained executable `f` with `V_x(f) >= V_x(p)` throughout the same preparation class, the informed benchmark is unchanged, `f` has no larger worst-case regret, and `f` has no smaller minimum fidelity. Subset inclusion gives the reverse optimum inequalities. The same representative therefore preserves the conjunction of a regret ceiling and a fidelity floor. This is an elementary order argument; S5 is evidence for the regret instrument, not a claimed source of the complete conjunction theorem.

Likewise, `E K_a = M_a E` and `mu = lambda E` yield

```text
mu K_a1 ... K_an = lambda M_a1 ... M_an E
```

by matrix multiplication. For substochastic kernels, stochastic `E` also preserves the represented total live mass. A failure of membership in this particular nonnegative emission family does not prove failure of every lower-dimensional task-value interface. These are different preservation targets, consistent with S1–S4 and the finite core's stated scope.

## 4. Attribution and contribution boundary

The manuscript can state that it **constructs and independently checks** a task-specific instance of these tools, including grammar-sensitive closure, exact convex history witnesses, positive-transport program dominance, and renewal/retention counterexamples. Their usefulness depends on whether the verified reductions and physical distinctions answer the operational question at an acceptable cost.

It cannot infer from this audit that the combined construction is historically first. It also should not demote the work merely because its instruments are classical. Correctness, efficiency, physical adequacy, historical priority and fertility are separate assessments. An exact adaptation that exposes a false shortcut or makes a previously uncheckable contract inspectable can matter without new abstract algebra.

Audit scope includes the linked maintenance, program-family, suffix-context and preparation-family proofs, and the v17 realization/retention report. It does not establish measured computational superiority, validity outside the stated finite grammar, universal benefit of renewal, physical accessibility of evaluator coordinates, or minimum representation dimension.
