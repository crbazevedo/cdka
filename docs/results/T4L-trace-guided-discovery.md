### Continuation-signature proposition

Let Q_A,Q_B be substochastic terminal-state kernels with a common full state space including resources. For initial measure mu, put Delta=mu(Q_B-Q_A). Equal survival says only Delta 1=0. A physically implementable common continuation with value v distinguishes them by Delta v.

Equality for every v in a declared continuation family F is equivalent to Delta annihilating span(F). For all initial laws, require (Q_B-Q_A)v=0 for every v in that span. This follows directly from linearity and point-mass initial measures.

### Continuation-weighted noise distance

For signal y let v_y(pi) be conditional continuation value and define nonnegative margin r_y(r)=max_pi v_y(pi)-v_y(D(r)). For m independent bit flips,

    R_y(p)=sum_r r_y(r) p^{d_H(E(y),r)}(1-p)^{m-d_H(E(y),r)}.

If some margin is positive, define

    d_y=min{d_H(E(y),r):r_y(r)>0},
    A_y=sum_{r:d_H(E(y),r)=d_y}r_y(r).

Then **R_y(p)=A_y p^{d_y}+O(p^{d_y+1})**. Expand the finite polynomials: lower powers vanish and the nonnegative first coefficients cannot cancel. If the zero-noise decision is already suboptimal, d_y=0; if all margins vanish, regret is identically zero.

For the middle diagnostic the task distance rises from 1 to 2. This adapts unequal error protection to executable continuation loss, not generic label recovery. It does not make the whole code a universal single-error-correcting code; other diagnostics have different distances or baseline compression loss.

## 5. Guard against causal overinterpretation

Two 80%-success policy marginals admit at least two cross-world couplings:
- Shared fault: both succeed .8 and both fail .2, with no success-switching entities.
- Disjoint faults: both succeed .6, only A .2, only B .2, with .2 rescued in each direction.

The observed marginals are identical. A rescued-trajectory fraction requires a structural model and declared shared exogenous variables. The expected contrasts above are identified under their fixed physical models; individual cross-world rescue claims are deliberately not inferred.
