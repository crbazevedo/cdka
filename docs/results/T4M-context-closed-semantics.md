# T4.M — Distinctions that survive permitted compositions

**2026-11-02 · Contribution:** a constructive, exact semantic-reduction and witness instrument adapted to the existing CDKA physical task, with separate certificates for linear continuations, stochastic state quotients and runtime observability. It generates necessary distinctions from operators rather than enumerating all paths. The algebraic backbone is established (weighted automata, invariant/Krylov spaces and probabilistic equivalence); the physical-interface adaptation, exact task comparisons and falsifiers are the executed CDKA contribution. Priority of any stronger generalization remains open.

## 1. Strong formulation of the user's insight

A difference is removable **relative to an explicit family of observations, actions, compositions, tasks and resource contracts** when no allowed future test can detect it. This is not the assertion that all combinatorial problems have small semantic quotients, nor that invariant topology alone decides computational complexity.

The useful property is not that a macrovariable remains constant. It is that its *evolution closes*: tomorrow's relevant description can be computed without recovering the discarded differences.

A large finite search is not automatically undecidable. A small explicit model may have exponentially many words but a small invariant response space; a succinctly specified model can still be expensive to construct, and exact arithmetic has a bit cost.

## 2. Contract: finite physical dynamics and declared observation interfaces

Let S be N live physical states. For each named primitive a, Q_a is a rational substochastic matrix. Missing row mass is absorbing failure. In the CDKA plant an unaffordable command has a zero row as a **fail-closed encoding of a non-admissible branch**; runtime policies still select only commands affordable at their observed resource balance.

Let G={g_1,...,g_r} be the terminal observables being certified. For survival, g=1 on live states. A chronological command word w=a_1...a_k has continuation value Q_w g=Q_(a_1)...Q_(a_k)g.

For control based on readable observations o, include diagonal masks P_o(s,s)=1{o(s)=o}, or equivalent action-observation kernels. These are tests of actual signals, not interventions or free sensors. A finite feedback tree is a sum of such masked branch products. Equal unmasked action-sequence values are **not** sufficient evidence for equal feedback performance.

Any bound on controller memory, complete truth-table libraries, command availability or protocol must remain attached to the policy class. Closure over all operator words can be a useful sufficient relaxation for a constrained library; it is not a proof that every word or contingent map is physically implementable. Regular protocol restrictions can be encoded by a finite protocol-state product before this analysis.

## 3. M1 — Generate the least continuation space, do not enumerate its paths

Define

    V_0 = span(G),
    V_(k+1) = span(V_k union {Q_a v: a in A, v in V_k}),
    V_* = span{Q_w g: w any finite permitted generator word, g in G}.

Once V_(k+1)=V_k, the space is invariant and the construction has stabilized. Each strict increase adds a dimension, so stabilization occurs after at most N-r_0 strict increases (r_0=dim V_0).

**Constructive algorithm:** retain only linearly independent generated vectors. Each retained vector carries an actual operator-word/probe witness. Apply each generator once to each accepted vector. If a candidate is dependent, linearity ensures every descendant is generated by applying the same operators to a combination of accepted vectors. No descendant needs separate path enumeration.

With d=dim V_*, at most |G|+|A|d candidates are evaluated. This is a count of matrix-vector/rank operations on an **explicit finite model**, not a universal sublinear or polynomial-in-succinct-input complexity claim. Exact rational coefficient growth and model construction remain costs.

If B has the accepted vectors as columns, the algorithm constructs exact matrices C_a and vectors c_g with

    Q_a B = B C_a,       g=B c_g.

**Proof:** every Q_a column(B) belongs to the certified invariant space, hence has unique coordinates in the independent basis. This is the intertwining certificate. Induction gives Q_w g=B C_w c_g. QED.

The C_a are linear coordinates; they need not be nonnegative stochastic matrices. A linear predictive realization is not automatically a physical finite-state controller.

## 4. M2 — Exact continuation equivalence and short witnesses

For two live measures mu,nu (not normalized after failure),

    mu ~ nu  iff  mu B=nu B
             iff  mu Q_w g=nu Q_w g for every declared generator word/probe.

The equivalence follows by the spanning identity. If signatures differ, one of the retained **actual continuation words** witnesses it. The BFS implementation supplies a shortest separating word in its generator alphabet: every earlier rejected vector was in the span of earlier accepted vectors. Observation masks have zero physical duration, so word length with masks is algebraic depth, not elapsed time.

The relation is closed under all declared generators. Adding a new action, sensor, task, or cost observable may refine it. A proposed extension R preserves the old linear signature if R B lies in span(B); otherwise the residual supplies an algebraic trigger for refinement. This is a candidate operational gate for changes to a supervisor's authority.

The v11 SERVICE→HOLD and SERVICE→SERVICE plans end with equal total live mass. Applying the generated basis to their complete terminal laws immediately finds a separating third command, HOLD: survival differs by .002062718070500702. A common REFRESH separates by .026369654670462817, reproducing the earlier trace finding.

Thus a tied endpoint score becomes either a **proof of equality for all continuations** or a concrete next experiment. No scalar endpoint fit alone can decide which case holds.

A second minimal witness uses four states x,y,p,q and two commands. PREPARE takes x→p and y→q; CHECK preserves x,y,p and fails at q. Every single command has the same survival from x and y. The shortest distinguishing word is PREPARE→CHECK (values 1 versus 0). Reversing its order gives 1 for both. Permuting operator order would destroy the relevant distinction.

## 7. M3 — A constructive multiplicity theorem, stronger than visible symmetry

Suppose each physical state is (s,z), with a task-relevant core s and an internal variable z. Assume actual commands and observations depend only on the core, the core-transition marginal is independent of z, and

    K_a((s,z),(s',z')) = Q_a(s,s') R_(a,s,s')(z,z'),

where every R_(a,s,s') is row-stochastic. Costs and failure events must also be core-relative. For L((s,z),s')=1{s=s'}, direct summation gives

    K_a L = L Q_a.

Every core-dependent observable and future masked continuation therefore factors through L for **any number of z states**. The internal z dynamics can be asymmetric and depend on both old and new s: a literal permutation symmetry is not required. The result is a semantic projection/fibration, proven by summing the nuisance channel before expanding the state space.

Executed instances with 1,2,5,16 and 64 nuisance states have 2,4,10,32 and 128 physical states. All retain rank 2, two core classes and five candidate evaluations. Row-sum certificates verify each explicit quotient. For k binary nuisance variables, the same algebra gives 2^(k+1) physical states but rank 2 under these hypotheses; that is a **derived factorized family**, not an enumerated performance benchmark at huge N.

Expose z to the policy, allow z to affect future costs, or change the core-transition marginal, and the conclusion requires a new certificate. An internal variable is not irrelevant merely because its immediate outcome is absent from the score.

## 8. Four falsifiers that improve the formulation

### F1. Open-loop equivalence is not feedback equivalence

Let W be a fair hidden bit and Z a readable bit. In one distribution Z=W; in the other Z is independent of W. Action u survives iff u=W. Every fixed action sequence has the same survival under both initial laws. The unobserved operator space has rank 2 and declares the laws equivalent.

Allow the *actual existing* sensor Z as a conditional input. The policy u=Z succeeds with probabilities 1 and 1/2. Adding observation masks grows the space to rank 4 and supplies a separating branch. The prior equivalence was valid for its declared interface, not for the richer one.

### F2. Low linear dimension is not few physical code states

For N states with distinct rational p_i, let each state survive a single step into one safe absorber with probability p_i. The continuation space is span{1,p}, of dimension 2. Nevertheless, all N pure-state signatures are different. Encoding these exact values as distinguishable binary labels needs ceil(log2 N) fixed bits; storing a scalar probability also requires precision. Algebraic coordinates are not free hardware registers.

### F3. Linear continuation equivalence need not give a Markov state quotient

In a five-state model u,v,r,x,y, take u→u; v→death; r→u with probability 1/2; x→r; y→u/v with probabilities 1/2 each. Starting from x or y, survival is (1,1,1/2,1/2,...) at every horizon. The linear signature merges x,y, with rank 3 overall. But their transition masses into the signature classes differ (one goes to r, the other to u/v). A stochastic quotient on those classes is invalid. The independent lumpability check rejects it; bisimulation-style refinement retains all five classes.

### F4. There is no universal collapse or complexity guarantee

An N-state deterministic chain that advances one step toward irreversible failure has N independent survival-continuation directions. Its dimension grows 1,2,...,N and all states remain distinct. For N=9, adjacent earliest states require eight steps to separate. A short trace cannot certify their equivalence.

The algorithm is polynomial in the size of the explicit rational linear model under appropriate arithmetic accounting, as in known weighted-automata methods; the quotient may still be as large as the model, the succinct model may expand exponentially, and optimal-policy synthesis may remain hard.

## 9. Semantic equivalence is not shared acceptable action

The earlier relation 'there exists an action safe for both contexts' is not generally transitive. With safe sets {a}, {a,b}, {b}, the first/second and second/third pairs are compatible but the first/third pair is not. It cannot be used directly as an equivalence partition.

Exact equality of all declared continuation responses *is* an equivalence. Compatibility hypergraphs remain appropriate for sufficiency of *some* decision; invariant signatures address equality of a declared future test family. Both structures are useful, but they solve different problems.

## 10. Research/oversight implication and precise next gate

A supervisor can operate on a macro-interface only if its observable safety properties and allowed future interventions factor through that interface, its update is realizable, and additional powers/sensors cannot silently invalidate the certificate. The method does not reduce real governance to a small assumed list of failure modes. It provides a way to **test** which distinctions are dispensable and produce a witness when a proposed abstraction is unsafe.

Next: let R11 provide a declared approximate tolerance and noisy physical update library; let R14 restrict jointly implementable observation-to-action maps. Search for a costed read/write architecture realizing the certified continuation signature. Do not hand an evaluator's hidden-state coordinate or a real-valued basis to the controller for free.
