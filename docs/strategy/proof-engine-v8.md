# CDKA Proof Engine v8 — Use a proof bridge, not another free mechanism

**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Active branches:** R11 and R14 only. This increment joins the v7 diagnostic to a mandatory physical memory transition and a degrading/maintainable command selector, while retaining the v6 terminal event. It does not claim to solve arbitrary recurrent upkeep.

## Strategic distillation

The object is the joint physical experiment-and-controller, not a naked information variable. Ask whether a diagnostic advantage survives transport, instruction execution, health correlation and pathwise resource reservation. A statistic is useful only relative to a declared future decision class; compressing for today's action does not establish tomorrow's sufficiency.

The appropriate next generalization is a **compositional interface certificate**, not another toy component. The current identities can evaluate arbitrary terminal joint laws. Their special scalar reduction requires a precise factorization; violating it should trigger the joint-law oracle, not a patch to marginal error rates.

## Tactics separated by mathematical function

**Algebra:** carry the unnormalized terminal law mu(H,S,M). For each M, compute the signed survival difference under each actual output channel. Optimize only over executable truth tables. The micro-oracle enumerates health changes, register erasure/flip, selector wear, late service and final driver faults.

**Optimization:** reserve the worst-case actual output cost before observing the diagnostic. An inverted HOLD may physically spend the REFRESH cost. Compare complete programs under the same deadline: skip, diagnosis, protected diagnosis, serviced selector, and both upgrades. A common lower-cost constant bypass remains available, so the overall optimum never becomes worse merely by adding an optional diagnostic.

**Calculus:** use partial derivatives of the *fixed architecture* margin, not a derivative through a policy switch. If U=[g-e(g+l)]_+ and a is retention, the pre-procedure surplus is [-theta(l-g)+a(1-2theta)U]/2. Derivatives show task-dependent complementarity between keeping a signal and reliably converting it to action. The envelope has kinks when optimal actions/programs change.

**Construction:** classify diagnostic outcomes by the sign of their decision advantage. The proof of an optimal binary encoder works because the downstream channel is symmetric and costs are outcome-independent; do not generalize to asymmetric shared-noise hardware without a new proof.

**Asymptotics:** for a stable world and only erasure, information stays positive at every finite n if rho<1, but actionable value may cross zero at a finite n when command flipping remains costly. Do not confuse this expiration of the decision margin with zero mutual information or a universal memory lifetime.

**Verification:** closed form versus independent joint-state propagation versus eight terminal truth tables; correlation adversary; durable action-trace bypass; same-payoff v7 nested limit; exact task-encoding enumeration. Fractions rather than Monte Carlo.

## What this model still assumes

A fixed physical terminal kernel, binary corrector health, a declared clock and affordable instructions, symptom k=0, independent noise for the scalar expression, a fixed-command bypass and a preinstalled library of truth tables. Knowledge about H and health of the mechanism acting on it are different variables. Neither is a runtime posterior handed in by the designer. Resources are tokens, not joules. A future autonomous-organization claim must close these boundaries rather than merely rename them.
