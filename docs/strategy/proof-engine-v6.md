# CDKA Proof Engine v6 — An executable research graph, not an expanding pile of toy models

**Purpose:** preserve alternatives, define when to resume them, and turn the current corrector/world/interface gaps into explicit proof obligations. No novelty claim follows from naming the program or combining known equations.

## Strategy

The candidate long-term contribution is a compositional certificate for physically implementable regulation under endogenous changes of observation, memory, action and upkeep. The object is a **single feasible physical policy family** and its viable trajectory laws. A theorem must state boundary, available information, resource/health dynamics, event, horizon and allowed reconfiguration.

Three outcomes count as progress: a new scoped theorem after antecedent audit; a verified counterexample that removes a misleading formulation; or a reusable classical instrument that materially reduces the next proof obligation. Only the first is labeled original.

Do not keep enlarging a toy model because the last one is easy to extend. If two scoped examples provide no new implication or separation, freeze feature work, audit predecessors and isolate the smallest unresolved statement. Preserve alternatives with a reactivation trigger and minimal test.

## Research memory and pivot mechanism

Canonical ledger: `registry/research-options.json`; readable map: `registry/research-options.md`. It records 32 branches and 14 rejected simplifications identified in the available corpus. Every branch has status, trigger, minimal experiment, tools, exit criterion, fallback branches and provenance. This is not an exhaustive scientific survey.

At most two branches are active. Next: **R11 hidden health/paid diagnostics** and **R14 coupled physical implementation costs**. Exact composition (R13), fallible corrector (R09), dynamic world (R10) and approximate transfer (R12) are validated only within their scope, not declared original.

When a gate fails: preserve the assertion and fixed causal contract; classify contradiction, implementation error, missing physical assumption, known antecedent, numerical instability or vacuous bound; follow a named fallback; change only the assumption under test; rerun the smallest positive and negative controls. Never change the task silently to rescue a claim.

## Work backwards

Target: synthesize a local physical policy in a smaller model and certify viability in the composed organization.

1. Which signals can the policy actually read at each decision boundary?
2. Is the reduced state an available signal or a designer-only statistic?
3. Do microstates in a block have identical action-conditioned laws? Otherwise, what uniform error is justified?
4. Does the lift preserve the whole implementability relation, not just action alphabets?
5. Are death and resource outcomes preserved rather than normalized away?
6. Does the claim concern finite-horizon probability or a closed recurrent support?
7. Does a failed assumption require observable refinement, quantified approximation or a new physical mechanism?

## Tools separated by function

**Algebra:** channel composition, absorbing failure completion, binomial convolution, symmetry and intertwining K L = L Kbar. Rational oracles certify finite equality.

**Analysis:** maximal coupling gives Delta_n=1-[product_i(1-delta_i)]^n under uniform conditional contracts. Bellman induction transfers a common implemented policy and, under matching policy classes, the optimum. Deployment regret is bounded by 2Delta_n, not by a claim of infinite safety.

**Calculus:** differentiate a fixed-policy matrix product. Local derivative contributions are weighted by forward exposure and backward viable continuation. Check against central differences; do not differentiate an argmax at an unexamined policy switch.

**Optimization:** readable-state Bellman synthesis; full microstate calculation only as an independently checked relaxation; LP/MILP/SAT for nonrectangular global prescription constraints; no free persistent random seed.

**Causal control:** explicit temporal ordering, paid acquisition after world drift, component damage and service. Correcting an old bit does not acquire current environmental information.

**Falsification:** break cell exchangeability, omit health, admit action-generated memory, perturb fault support, change mean to worst-context objectives, and retain failed mass.

**Anteriority:** lumpability and compositional stochastic abstractions already exist. Compare against Buchholz; Lavaei/Soudjani/Zamani; Junges et al.; nonrectangular robust MDPs. A small exact quotient is an instrument, not a new theory.

## Executed reformulations

A changing world and fallible maintained corrector admit a readable quotient in the specified fatal-command-error model. The 80-to-20 reduction is exact under exchangeability and fails under heterogeneous cell errors. New sensing and copying separate knowledge freshness from data integrity. Maintenance becomes preferable when future corrector health matters. Finite-time approximation contracts can certify transfer, but their validity depends on the policy interface and horizon. Perfectly readable health remains an explicit trusted interface and is not disguised as a solved problem.

## Continue/stop rule

Next relax health observability and impose a joint selector-realization constraint, one at a time. Do not add neural networks, more oscillators or continuous thermodynamics merely to enlarge the model. Those approaches remain recorded in R24/R27/R32 with triggers. If G4 yields only a restatement of an existing FSC theorem, retain it as an instrument and reconsider the smallest unresolved joint-maintenance condition before G5.
