# Program-family pruning: an accepted, bounded R11/R14 advance

**4 November 2026.** Parent checkpoint:
`5972303fb8728fe0d0bbea9beed796c8209f9b40`.

## Decision and larger purpose

The previous reachable-diagnosis result reduced the evaluator to four signed
coordinates while still constructing every complete program. This increment
crosses the next gate: it certifies the exclusion of program families before
generating their members and demonstrates a total-cost advantage on a declared
regime. The acquisition, hidden health, actual voter, D/R/M operations,
observation boundary and mandatory-work grammar are unchanged. Only R11/R14
remain active.

The accepted method is conditional, rather than a universal replacement for
enumeration. Its value is a constructive connection between the physically
reachable domain, a preserved response order and an independently checkable
program cover. The [research memory](research-memory.md) records the decisions
that produced this result; the [article](../articles/program_families.md)
develops its interpretation.

## What was prepared and executed

The preparation contrast has the form `-x E` with `x>=0`. For D/R, the checked
identities `E K_a=A_a E` have nonnegative A. Therefore componentwise dominance
of four-coordinate responses survives every D/R prefix. A rejected response
can be replaced by one retained **actual complete program** for every
preparation in the cone.

Maintenance requires a different boundary. Every no-M suffix response remains
in sixteen physical coordinates. A verified four-by-sixteen map `B=E K_M`
forms its maintenance seed without assuming that M preserves the four-coordinate
domain. The recurrence combines these seeds with D/R children of the preceding
frontier. Its supplied dominance assignments cover every candidate; induction
covers every legal word omitted before construction. The checker verifies the
cover without rediscovering a Pareto frontier.

The resulting representatives are executable words from the original library.
They are neither convex mixtures of programs nor free coordinatewise response
envelopes. They preserve informed per-history maxima, optimal common minimax
regret, optimal maximin fidelity and existence of a program satisfying both
regret and fidelity limits under the same prepaid reservation. Actual execution
cost may differ between representatives and replaced programs.

## Exact evidence and preserved controls

The exact sweep checks 3,192 complete-program dominance relations over
K=0,1,2,4,6,8, compares 53 retained responses with full physical propagation,
and reproduces all 384 parent-fixture values. The principal K=4 result remains
DRDDM with regret `0.0025321961178476887...`; the joint gate of regret at most
`0.003` and fidelity at least `0.85` remains impossible.

At K=8 there are 2,560 legal complete programs. The method constructs 286 final
candidates and 256 no-M suffixes: 542 distinct complete programs, with 2,018
never generated by the pruned method and 17 representatives retained. The
representative count alone would substantially understate the work performed.

The zero-repair and uninformative-sensor controls remain exact. At sensor error
one half all original programs tie, demonstrating why preserving one optimum
does not preserve every identity. An early-maintenance counterexample also
remains: after preparation DDDD, MR exceeds RM for the K=1 workload. Moving every
maintenance call to the end is therefore an invalid shortcut.

## Cost gate: accepted where it pays

Both methods return informed values, one minimax program and one maximin
program. The enumerator receives the same suffix sharing, four-coordinate
prefix algebra and four-by-sixteen maintenance contraction. Both use the same
compact decision certificate; neither is charged for producing prediction
envelopes. The pruned method additionally pays its family checker.

## Rejected methods remain part of the result

Physical Bellman, signed-state and hybrid branch-and-bound probes produced
sound exclusions but lost total time against their shared four-coordinate
baseline. At K=6, the hybrid avoided constructing 439 of 512 complete programs
yet still paid more for repeated bounds and checking. These are single
fresh-run controls, not the repeated benchmark used for acceptance. They
remain an explicit rejected route rather than disappearing behind the winning
method.
[K4 control](../data/program-family-bound-control-k4.json) ·
[K6 control](../data/program-family-bound-control-k6.json)

Two operational lessons now govern further work. Give the baseline every safe
algebraic improvement available to the candidate, and match the requested
output before interpreting speed. A compact certificate for informed values,
minimax and maximin does not by itself answer joint regret/fidelity feasibility;
that answer requires the retained-matrix scan. The cover makes the scan sound
for the original library.

## One next obligation

All `2^K` no-M suffix seeds still remain. The next gate is to avoid constructing
them using a certified relation that survives ordinary suffix extension and
the `E M` boundary. Four-coordinate closure through M remains false. Any new
relation must preserve an actual feasible representative or a valid explicit
bound, cover omitted suffixes without enumerating them, and beat the accepted
implementation after construction and checking costs.

Preparation enumeration `2^h` remains open. This step does not open a second
physical model or a separate history-pruning experiment. A fixed contrast
dimension is not a polynomial complexity bound. Full prediction envelopes,
all tied optima and execution-cost Pareto frontiers also remain outside this
response-only cover. If the next suffix relation cannot satisfy its proof and
total-cost obligations, the resulting obstruction is the deliverable.
