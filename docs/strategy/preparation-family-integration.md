## Decision

Accept exact convex preparation coverage as a verified finite-family mechanism
and as a conditional full-cost improvement in the demonstrated larger cases.
Keep the strongest streamed reference for small cases. End this sequence of
compiler scaling probes here: the next default research task concerns the
physical validity of a representation when retention and fresh acquisition
become competing capabilities.

The central research interest is **how a task-relevant distinction becomes a
physically usable decision, and which changes make the old representation
insufficient**. This increment advances that interest by closing both sides of
one common decision: uncertain preparations and feasible complete programs.
Its contribution is a proved and implemented coupling with exact checks and a
measured regime of advantage. Historical priority is a separate question; no
general novelty or new autonomous-agent claim follows from this result.

## What is now integrated

The inherited acquisition and ordinary physics yield a positive four-coordinate
signature x with source fidelity `V_x(p)=1/2-x q(p)`. The existing program cover
retains actual executable words that dominate every omitted legal program.
The new preparation cover retains actual histories whose convex hull equals
that of every original history signature. Its recurrence generates only D/R
children of retained parents. Every discard has an exact nonnegative witness
whose weights sum to one.

The composed checker begins with the raw physical kernels, initial law,
terminal observable and horizons. It verifies the program cover, verifies the
preparation cover or streams the full grammar, then checks every retained
program's universal regret/fidelity bounds and independently propagated
attaining histories. A digest binds the data; the identities and complete
coverage establish correctness.

This preserves one minimax program, one maximin program and the existence of
one common program meeting both a regret ceiling and fidelity floor. The
informed benchmark and the chosen program always use the same preparation.
Informed values are evaluated as exact queries; they are not interpolated
from endpoint maxima. [Proof](../results/preparation-family-proof.md) ·

## Cost changes the adoption decision

Both methods receive the same verified two-amplitude/trailing-D specialization
and the same program compiler at each K. The reference streams in depth-first
order, creates history strings only for changed attainers and retains one
value row. It does not build or export the full preparation table.

Each of three observations per method/case uses a fresh process. Timed work
includes physical construction, acquisition, encoding, program and preparation
discovery, cold numerical imports and first solver use when needed, compact
decisions, complete JSON serialization/parsing, independent checking, an
informed history query, joint-gate scans and compact answer serialization.

## Lessons that now constrain the next experiment

1. **A representation can preserve a decision without reproducing the full
   physical law.** The preparation hull is in contrast-signature space. Do not
   silently promote it to a law-level mixture or to readable controller memory.
2. **Coverage requires the right algebra.** A conic identity with mass below
   one falsely raises a physically attainable worst fidelity. A convex witness
   needs both exact coordinates and coefficient sum one.
3. **Optimization is not affine.** Every fixed program value interpolates;
   the maximum over programs generally does not. Preserve common-input
   differences and evaluate the informed envelope directly.
4. **Attainment does not prove optimality.** One worst history supports one
   program's extremum. Every remaining candidate must be bounded to certify
   a common optimum or infeasibility.
5. **An arithmetic discovery belongs to the comparison.** The two-amplitude
   codec improves both methods. It depends on a trailing-D index and verified
   physical identities; the hull has affine dimension four by h=3.
6. **Stop once the declared gate is resolved.** The successful larger case
   and the adverse small case are both preserved. Further hull timing at still
   larger horizons does not address the next physical question.

## One next gate: acquisition competes with retention

The source-fidelity compiler has **16 physical states** and fixed D/R
preparations followed by a fixed complete program. Main's
[recurrent-maintenance loop](../results/T4Q-recurrent-maintenance-loop.md)
has a **different 64-state contract**: stored diagnosis, hidden health and a
finite resource balance, with a shared faulty bank selecting maintenance and
performing work. Its policy-uniform twelve-phase stochastic-emission
factorization is an evaluator interface. Its survival metric and resource
accounting do not transfer to source fidelity.

The next task follows the [v16 compass](learning-compass-v16.md): make one
controlled change in that recurrent model so fresh report acquisition competes
with physical retention at the declared equal resource price. Preserve the
existing hidden causes, physical selector, resource guard and task sequence;
write the changed observation/retention contract before deriving any reduction.

The testable claim is whether all allowed policies still share one emission
interface E with `K_pi=A_pi E` and `E K_pi=M_pi E`. A positive result must verify
the identities for every admitted action/policy and preserve failure mass. A
negative result must give an exact physical law or continuation witnessing the
lost report/health dependence, then add only the distinction that witness
forces. A free belief state, free history label or ideal selector is not a
repair of the physical model.

The complete experiment must compare always-refresh, always-retain and the
inherited physically executable controls under the same resource prices. It
must report viability, resource trajectories and the cost of the evaluator,
with an independent raw-state oracle. A new distinction, a proved surviving
interface, or a justified negative result resolves the gate. Another faster
offline table alone does not.

This is a prepared next obligation. No acquisition/retention extension or
physical autonomy claim is reported as completed in this increment.
