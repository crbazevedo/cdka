## Decision

Accept the finite-horizon suffix cover for the demonstrated larger cases,
conditional on the unchanged physical and output contract. Keep the strengthened
eight-feature parent as the measured choice for the smaller cases. This is a
measured regime decision, not a universal horizon threshold or automatic
solver dispatcher.

The result has two independently useful parts. A fixed eight-row signed dual
observer replaces full 16 suffix arithmetic; both methods receive it. Nested
context cones then allow the candidate to discard suffixes with a common
physical representative and exact coverage through the maintenance boundary.
The observer's signed matrices alone supply no positive suffix order.

## What was established

The checker verifies the observer in all sixteen physical coordinates, the
nonnegative four-coordinate ordinary transports, the exact maintenance bridge,
every initial context identity, finite closure with birth-level index bounds,
and every suffix and complete-program cover assignment. It uses exact rational
arithmetic and does not discover cones or Pareto frontiers. Optional numerical
optimization proposes support only during discovery. Missing or inaccurate
proposals increase work rather than authorizing an invalid equality.

At K=14, the final step constructs 44 suffix candidates, retains 24 suffixes,
forms 116 outer candidates and constructs 160 distinct complete word names.
It retains 53 complete-program representatives. The same-algebra parent
constructs 32,860 of the 262,144 legal complete words. Thus 32,700 additional
complete words avoid construction in the final level. The certificate itself
uses 422 context generators. These are distinct work and representation counts;
the full per-level record remains available.

The principal four-preparation/K=4 matrix reproduces all 384 inherited values.

gate remains impossible. The exact sweep also checks 120 complete physical
responses and their dominance witnesses at small horizons, 170 retained
full 16 responses, 340 two-way frontier comparisons against the stronger
reference, and 53 retained-response agreements with the full 16 parent.

required for a complete declared decision are timed. The candidate's compiler
includes cold NumPy/SciPy imports, first solver initialization, numerical
proposals and exact reconstruction; no precompiled cone enters the timed path.
The baseline pays the same observer improvement and checks its own family
certificate. The recorded environment is Python 3.12.14, NumPy 2.3.5 and SciPy 1.17.0.

## Adverse evidence retained

1. **The original order fails at the boundary.** After the physical preparation
   D^32, prepending M reverses the ranking of RDDRRD and RDRDDR. Preserve this
   witness whenever an implementation attempts to reuse E-only suffix order.
2. **An unbounded promise has a cost.** Every closed invariant context cone
   containing EM forces equality on both degraded contrast responses. Exact
   finite counts give at least 67 required classes at K=8 and 272 at K=12 for that
   universal order. Finite ladders retain 9 and 19 suffixes. This does not exclude
   all unbounded pruning or establish asymptotic growth.
3. **Incomplete proof structure can be unsound.** The scratch checker accepted
   an empty context list via a truncated zip. The resulting candidate set
   failed to cover MR. Production rejects empty and truncated witnesses,
   incorrect shapes/types, missing transitions and future-level dependencies.
4. **Uniform approximation compressed too little.** The exact span-error
   control's tight tolerance retained nearly every suffix. Preserve its
   historical observations with their weaker timing protocol, outside the
   central comparison.

## The one next gate

The remaining explicit exponential axis is preparation histories. Attempt a
certificate for their reachable family under the same acquisition and ordinary
transports, while keeping the now-accepted program compiler as the reference.
No experiment on that axis is executed in this increment.

A possible exact starting recurrence is

```
P_0 = {x_acquisition},
P_h = conv(P_(h-1) A_D union P_(h-1) A_R).
```

Every retained vertex must correspond to an actual history; a discarded
preparation signature may be covered by an exact convex combination of
signatures of retained physical histories. The equality is in the inherited
task-sufficient contrast representation. This concerns uncertainty over physical preparations and
does not authorize a mixed or history-dependent executable controller. A
certificate must retain the same-history comparator differences used by
minimax regret and the absolute fidelity floor.

**Output obligation comes first.** The next comparison must request a compact
common decision, its exact value, attaining preparation witnesses and joint
feasibility. Enumerating every informed value B_h for all 2^h original histories
has an output-size cost of 2^h even if the decision can be computed compactly.
Implicit/queryable informed values and an explicit exponential list are
different output contracts. Give both methods the same chosen contract.

The informed envelope `B(x)=max_p V_x(p)` is generally convex and piecewise
affine. A convex decomposition of x does not authorize averaging its informed
values. Preserve regret by maximizing the same-preparation differences
`V_x(q)-V_x(p)` and answer an individual informed query by evaluating the
certified program frontier at that x. Such a query still pays to read and
propagate the requested history.

Convex witnesses must have nonnegative coefficients summing to one; conic
coverage alone is insufficient. Witnesses must refer to the final retained
histories or a separately checked acyclic decomposition. A single attaining
history establishes a selected program's worst case, but optimality or joint
infeasibility can need different witnesses against different candidate programs.
The reference must stream four-coordinate preparation histories and return the
same compact output, without being charged for an unrequested full value table.

Price discovery, exact combination recovery, horizon closure, query/decision
witnesses, rational growth, serialization and independent checking. Do not
infer a bounded number of extreme preparations from four coordinates. Accept
only a sound certificate with a measured full-cost improvement, or preserve a
concrete obstruction. This becomes the next active obligation after the
current increment is checked and committed.
