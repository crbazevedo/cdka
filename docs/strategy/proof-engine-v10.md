## Tools separated by purpose

**Algebra:** nonnegative joint signal-health masses, killed survival vectors, physical channel composition and global decoder-image constraints. A plan chosen by a table is not an unpriced runtime pointer; the received word remains its physical selector state.

**Geometry:** physical Hamming neighbors are not arbitrary abstract names. Preserve channel automorphisms when applying symmetry reductions. Optimize weighted confusion costs, not uniform reconstruction error by default.

**Optimization:** cardinality-bounded portfolio of complete plans, exact enumeration of small encoders, paid library provisioning, nonrectangular whole-controller constraints. A per-output Bayes maximum is valid only after checking the shared template limit.

**Calculus/analysis:** every two-bit code value is quadratic in p. For a fixed code, derivative identifies sensitivity; the maximum is a piecewise envelope with switches. Exact minima of difference quadratics certify a whole interval, avoiding unsupported interpolation.

**Constructive computation:** retain both a winning encoder/table and a certificate against alternatives. Do not claim a physical resource lower bound from an arbitrary slot price. Do not conflate occupied diagnostic symbols, actual reachable storage states, fixed-width bits and mutual information.

**Prior art:** Walrand–Varaiya causal coding; De Micheli–Brayton–Sangiovanni-Vincentelli state assignment; Poupart–Boutilier value-directed compression; FSC synthesis. Their primary records/abstracts were inspected. A systematic novelty theorem-by-theorem audit remains pending.

## Dead-end routing

- Alphabet minimization loses noisy control: preserve physical geometry and activate joint coding, R11/R14.
- Formal labels hide actuator/resource traces: include all accessible signals, R15/R35.
- Equal-price hull pruning fails under real costs: cost-aware library and global program synthesis, R14/R28.
- Noise is nonexchangeable/correlated: replace the symmetric polynomial with the actual channel, R08; do not average away dependence.
- Two-epoch exact gain is too small for hardware costs: report a tolerance frontier and keep the cheaper code; R17/R37.
- An update requires discarded Y: reject it by the v9 congruence witness; R13/R11, not a free posterior.
- Effort units are interpreted physically without calibration: R27/R05.
