# CDKA Proof Engine v4 — Implementability, complementarity and funded fidelity

**2026-11-01.** This increment executes the R1/R2 obligations of v3 in a minimal controlled family. Proven model-specific statements are separated from unestablished originality. Earlier corpus files remain intact.

## Strategic distillation

The primitive is a **jointly implementable policy family** Phi(Z,R), not the Cartesian product of independently available actions. The target is one common controller that maintains the organization, conveys usable distinctions and sustains the physical fidelity required by the intended lifetime.

Two independent witnesses are insufficient at three levels:
1. Available commands do not imply a contingent selector can realize their combination.
2. Faithful writing does not help if the input-dependent selector does not transmit the relevant distinction.
3. A regenerative resource cycle does not imply indefinitely reliable operation at finite error/effort.

## Separate tools by function

**Algebra / causality:** channels, whole-prescription feasible sets, global row coupling, no missing physical side channels. Exact Fraction oracles for selected finite families.

**Optimization:** maximize linear contrasts over a polytope (LP, dual certificate), nonconvex finite resource allocation (integer-grid DP), upper concave envelope (continuous certificate). Never turn a grid optimum into an unsupported continuum proof.

**Calculus:** derive the sign of ell'', solve one scalar tangency equation, invert q for high reliability, compare asymptotic regimes. These tools reveal when optimization should concentrate rather than spread scarce resources.

**Temporal viability:** propagate live mass; compare robust context profiles, not just average success. Resource updates occur only on live trajectories. Infinite lifetime results require one fixed feasible policy, not a new design for every n.

**Physical calibration:** our effort law is stipulated. To link least action, derive two actual conditional error laws, their common endpoints/deadlines, correlation, and attainable actions. Exponents alone omit finite-noise prefactors. No conversion to joules is asserted.

## Next hypotheses, deliberately beyond this toy family

**H-structure:** Under a finite resource/health state, adding a reconfiguration action can create a new persistent safe information channel. Seek the minimum resource/authority making a previously empty observation-consistent recurrent kernel nonempty. Use support synthesis, MILP/SAT and controlled-bisimulation quotients. Compare with existing POMDP/controller synthesis before novelty claims.

**H-correlation:** Shared substrate noise couples selector and write failure. The product of contrasts and equal-split law can fail. Derive exact two-error joint laws and test whether correlation helps (error cancellation) or hurts (common-mode failure) without changing the causal task. Use coupling polyhedra and Frechet bounds before large-deviation refinements.

**H-redundancy:** Distinguish making each event arbitrarily unlikely from making events nonfatal via redundancy and recovery. This could change the n log n law and the constant-flux no-go; neither result is universal.

**H-policy budget:** Costs of a complete conditional prescription should not be priced as independent costs of rows never jointly implementable. Search finite controller libraries with identical action sets but different realization costs, and derive exact dual certificates of the resulting gap.
