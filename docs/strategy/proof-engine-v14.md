## General methodology refinement

A semantic quotient is valid only for the contexts that can read its boundary. Under stochastic composition, retain the joint law of every accessible signal, conditioned on the readable input, and any latent context governing later correlated noise. Marginals and noiseless truth are not sufficient.

Equal joint laws, legal extension grammar and remaining resources imply equal continuation values through common kernels. When those contracts change, re-certify. A full joint law may be reducible further for a restricted task family; that is a new proof obligation.
