## Dead-end routing

- Rank saturates at full N: R13 task/interface refinement or R12 quantified approximation, not arbitrary merging.
- A new sensor breaks equality: R15/R35 include the actual signal in the physical cut; do not insist the old quotient remains safe.
- A state-signature partition is not lumpable: preserve the linear representation or refine stochastically. Never fabricate macro transition probabilities.
- A basis is inaccessible to runtime: R11 construct physical memory; R14 price its implementation.
- Tiny rank but hard optimization: retain the reduced oracle and analyze policy constraints; do not infer that combinatorial hardness has vanished.
- Full model too large to construct: apply conditional-stochastic factor elimination or verified symmetries before explicit expansion; use R28 only after the structural contract is established.
