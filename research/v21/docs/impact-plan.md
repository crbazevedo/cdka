# V21 decision — a certificate-driven portfolio, not one universal solver

7 November2026, São Paulo. R11/R14 remain the foundational active proof branches. This is a competitive instrument.

## Decision executed

Broadened v20 beyond task-relative commutation by using rank-one transient regeneration. Work/reset order changes the answer, but run-length histograms preserve it. Implemented exact weights, counterexamples, an informed subset/renewal alternative and new official Storm comparisons including strong bisimulation. No expanded global product is built by the specialization.

## What changed our choices

- Dependence is not synonymous with a global representation. The correct residual history may be a count, a histogram, an algebraic signature or a small interface.
- Histogram evaluation costs p(H+1), not a polynomial inH. The informed subset method costs2^N; it wins in some measured regimes. Keep both and choose the expensive axis structurally.
- Every source transformation must preserve timing, failure, absorption and common noise. A false fast answer is not a contribution.
- CI initially masked benchmark errors. Preserve the failure, fix API/pipeline gates and inspect underlying values before quoting time ratios.
- A positive narrow result deserves investment without pretending it already establishes broad SOTA.

## Promotion plan

G1 executed: certified new class, positive implementation, independent exact oracles, raw official measurements and defeats.
G2: predeclare a cost/conditioning selector between histogram and subset-renewal. Calibrate on one family and evaluate held-out models, counting parsing, construction, cold/warm queries, RSS and output accuracy. Do not choose retrospectively after knowing each winner.
G3: extract these contracts conservatively from a broader PRISM/JANI frontend and measure external coverage/rejection. Integrate with a mature solver as a certified preprocessor/fallback portfolio.

Next theorem candidate: a rank-r regeneration interface with controlled order interactions. Rank alone is not enough; the partial-reset1/64 witness forbids a naive extension. First prove closure and its actual structural cost, then extend benchmarks.

Manuscript candidate: Certified Regenerative Exposure Compilation for Heterogeneous Deadline Verification. Central result: a source-checkable rank-one reset contract yields exact partition-indexed Markov aggregation. Companion: a dual2^N renewal algorithm and a measured structural portfolio. Originality audit is at this exact formulation, not dismissed because its ingredients are known.

Progress means a class solved, a guarantee proved, a matching competitor defeated, a meaningful failure or a lower real compilation cost. More versions alone are not progress.
